Know your website!

This website is an effort to visualize crime data in a clean, intuitive, and interactive format. Every time you visit, you'll be able to seamlessly explore crime statistics and understand patterns based on data aggregated from multiple reliable news platforms.
Studying crime rates across Indian states or union territories helps evaluate whether crime is rising or falling in a specific area. If an increase is observed, authorities can investigate the reasons behind the surge and implement focused actions to help reduce the crime rate effectively.

Know your data sources!


We have collected the crime data from various resources.

  • The historic data used for training the model has been taken from Kaggle.
  • We have used web scraping to fetch crime data from news websites.
